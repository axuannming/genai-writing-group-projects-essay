# Generative AI Writing Tools in Hong Kong College Group Projects

An academic research essay examining how unrestricted use of generative AI writing tools affects team fairness, academic integrity, critical thinking, and collaborative skill development in Hong Kong university group assignments.

## Contents
- `research_essay.md` — Clean Markdown text of the full essay and references
- `research_essay.docx` — Anonymized Word document (author/group metadata scrubbed)
- `research_essay.pdf` — Clean PDF export

## Key Arguments & Findings
- **Team Fairness & Free-Riding**: Argues that AI-generated sections conceal lack of genuine contribution, increase revision burdens for conscientious teammates, and expose entire groups to academic integrity penalties.
- **Survey Evidence**: Draws on a group-administered survey (`n = 22` responses; raw survey spreadsheet excluded for respondent privacy) indicating widespread concern about AI detection and copy-paste practices.
- **Cognitive & Skill Erosion**: Warns that outsourcing analysis and drafting weakens students' independent reasoning and authentic teamwork skills.
- **Policy Recommendations**: Proposes clear university boundaries between permitted editing and prohibited generation, mandatory process logs and AI usage declarations, and oral presentation/defense components in group projects.
